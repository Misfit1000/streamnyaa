## Changes

- Discovery considers up to thirteen distinct anime from airing, upcoming and established catalog groups, rather than six. Established candidates rotate through bounded provider pages. News leads can be up to seven days old instead of forty-eight hours.
- Publication dates include JSON-LD and article timestamps. A fresh dated report that directly links a title-specific official statement can corroborate an undated official page. The reporting date is never assigned to the official source. A feed headline/date alone remains insufficient.
- Useful concise announcements no longer require an invented dramatic consequence or an extra context sentence. Headings and length follow the evidence; factual attribution, originality, sensitive-claim confirmation and anti-filler checks remain.
- When news is absent, covered or rejected, the worker researches release/franchise guides before considering a distinct activity report. Guides require substantial title-specific official information plus attributed catalog context. They answer a concrete format, release or franchise question without claiming a viewing review, full watch order, unannounced availability or breaking news.
- Guide claims have stable per-installment identities. Score changes, fresh retrieval times and altered headlines do not manufacture another guide. Discovery can continue beyond its first candidate batch when earlier candidates are already reserved.
- Official HTML page titles are retained when image logos leave the body without the anime name; substantive body evidence is still required. Matching normalizes punctuation and spacing without removing installment numbers. Known HTTP official links are attempted over HTTPS only, and an alternate listed official site can be checked with checkpointed progress. Manual jobs can select a different bounded established-title catalog page; its saved page remains stable on retry.
- One failing news provider does not erase collected evidence or prevent research using healthy independent sources. Denied/throttled hosts are not repeatedly requested within the worker. Saved checkpoints, retry deadlines and provider-specific warnings remain explicit.
- Version-4 guides and reports use the same cited body in private preview and initial/public HTML. Guide labels survive archive summaries. Calculated activity figures, coverage, duplicate-period and comparable-period safeguards are retained.
- Guide instructions select useful distinctions rather than exhaustive credits. Cited percentage claims must retain the exact value and unit from their own source; foreign-language jokes and fractions cannot become literal percentages. Dense staff/cast rosters are rejected for the bounded revision.
